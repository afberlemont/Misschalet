# Playbook: monthly review (`/mois`)

Read `state/settings.md` (phase) and follow that phase's section only.

## Phase 1: the month in 10 minutes
1. **Regularity**: posts published vs. planned (from `content/calendar.md`).
   That is the phase-1 measure.
2. **What she noticed**: from the 4 weekly notes in `analytics/weekly/`:
   questions asked, booking requests, posts people liked.
3. **Keep, change, drop**: one decision per item, at most 3 (a format, a
   theme, a day). Record a rule in `state/decisions.md` only if it lasts.
4. **Next 4 weeks**: `editorial-planner` extends `content/calendar.md`.
5. **Phase 2?** If the criteria of `os/phases.md` are met, tell her what
   phase 2 would add, in 3 lines, and let her decide (with the agency). Do
   not push it.
Write `analytics/monthly/<YYYY-MM>.md` (10 lines max).

## Phase 2 and later
Reference: `os/PROPERTY_GROWTH_ORCHESTRATOR.md` §13 and §15. Output:
`analytics/monthly/<YYYY-MM>.md` and, only if the change is meaningful, an
updated `strategy/growth-strategy.md`.
1. **Evidence** (parallel): `analytics-measurement` (vs. baseline, channels,
   organic, booking funnel, top and weak content; business first),
   `seo-search` (keyword opportunities), `research-intelligence` (competitors,
   coming season, new questions), `social-community` (monthly learning),
   `viral-video-analyst` (at most 3 formats to try), `partnerships` (status).
2. **Decide**: every campaign keep / change / stop against its KPI and review
   date; at most 3 experiments (hypothesis, metric); seasonal priorities.
3. **Plan**: `editorial-planner` (campaigns, calendar), `blog-writer`
   (articles), ideas triage including system ideas (`os/evolution.md`).
4. **Present** in French: the month in 5 numbers with their meaning; keep,
   stop, try; what she must decide. Update `state/decisions.md` and
   `state/current-priorities.md`.
