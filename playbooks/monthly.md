# Playbook: monthly strategy review (`/mois`)

Once a month. Reference: `os/PROPERTY_GROWTH_ORCHESTRATOR.md` sections 13
and 15. Output: `analytics/monthly/<YYYY-MM>.md` and, only if the change is
meaningful, an updated `strategy/growth-strategy.md`.

## 1. Evidence (parallel subagents)
- `analytics-measurement`: performance vs. baseline; channel contribution;
  organic growth; booking funnel (site visits → booking page → booking or
  inquiry); top and weak content. Business first: bookings, revenue,
  occupancy, direct-booking share, then the rest.
- `seo-search`: keyword opportunities refreshed; pages to create or improve.
- `research-intelligence`: competitor movement, the coming season (events,
  holidays, demand), new guest questions.
- `social-community`: the monthly social learning (formats, themes, audience).
- `viral-video-analyst`: formats to try next month (from `references/` and
  `intelligence/video-formats.md`), at most 3.
- `partnerships`: status of partnerships, next steps.

## 2. Decide (orchestrator)
For every active campaign: objective, primary and secondary KPI, baseline,
direction, review date; keep, change or stop. Choose next month's experiments
(at most 3, each with a hypothesis and a metric). Refresh seasonal priorities.

## 3. Plan
`editorial-planner`: next month's campaigns and calendar. `blog-writer`: next
month's articles. Ideas triage (`playbooks/ideas.md`), including system ideas
for the agency (`os/evolution.md`).

## 4. Present (in French)
One screen: the month in 5 numbers with their meaning, what we keep, what we
stop, what we try, what she must decide. Record decisions in
`state/decisions.md`; update `state/current-priorities.md`.
